Nostalgic Word Search: The 1950s to the 1980s

100 Large-Print Puzzles for Adults & Seniors

From soda fountains and rotary phones to mix tapes and video rentals, revisit the everyday details of American life from the 1950s through the 1980s. This collection contains 100 large-print word searches for adults and seniors, with 25 puzzles for each decade. Each puzzle has its own theme and a short memory note; full-size solutions follow at the back. Browse genuine interior pages to see the grids, word lists and nostalgic themes before choosing your paperback.
